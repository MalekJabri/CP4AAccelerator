## 5. TRUSTEE POWERS

The Trustees shall have the following powers without restriction:

1. **Investment Powers:** Invest in any asset, including equities, bonds, real property, private equity, and alternative investments, without restriction and without being limited to trustee investment standards.
2. **Distribution Powers:** Distribute income and/or capital to any Beneficiary at their absolute discretion.
3. **Accumulation Powers:** Accumulate income for up to 21 years from the date of settlement.
4. **Advancement Powers:** Advance up to 100% of capital to any Beneficiary.
5. **Delegation:** Delegate investment management to IBK Wealth Management Ltd (Discretionary Investment Manager).

---

## 6. PROTECTOR'S POWERS

The Protector may (but is not obliged to):
- Veto additions to or exclusions of Beneficiaries.
- Require removal and replacement of Trustees.
- Veto any distribution in excess of GBP 500,000 in any single transaction.

---

## 7. TRUSTEE REMUNERATION

IBK Trustees Ltd shall be entitled to charge fees in accordance with its published Trust Administration Fee Schedule (effective 2024), which shall not exceed 0.35% per annum on the value of the Trust Fund.
