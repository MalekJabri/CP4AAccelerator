## 6. MARKET COMMENTARY

Q4 2024 saw continued resilience in global equity markets, driven primarily by strong US technology earnings and an anticipated ECB rate cut cycle. European equities lagged US peers due to persistent manufacturing weakness in Germany and France. Fixed income tightened modestly as credit spreads compressed. The portfolio's overweight to global equities (particularly US technology) was the primary contributor to outperformance.

**Q1 2025 Outlook:** We maintain a neutral-to-positive view on global equities with selective exposure to European rate-sensitive sectors. We intend to modestly increase the fixed income allocation to capture yield in the 3–5 year segment of the EUR curve.
