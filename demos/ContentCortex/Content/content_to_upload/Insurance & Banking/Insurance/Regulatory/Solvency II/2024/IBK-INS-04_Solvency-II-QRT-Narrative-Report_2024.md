## 7. ORSA CONCLUSIONS (SUMMARY)

The 2024 ORSA confirmed that IBK Life Insurance SA maintains an adequate solvency position under all stress scenarios tested, including:
- **Interest rate stress (+200 bps):** SCR Coverage drops to 154% (above 150% appetite threshold).
- **Mortality shock (+15%):** SCR Coverage drops to 168%.
- **Combined stress:** SCR Coverage drops to 138% — within contingency planning threshold.

Management actions available (suspension of dividends, reinsurance activation) would restore coverage to above 155% under all scenarios.
