## 2. NATURE OF SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY

**Summary of Concern:**

The IBK transaction monitoring system flagged a series of unusual transactions on a corporate current account (Account type: SME Current Account, Currency: EUR) over the period **1 July 2024 – 12 August 2024**. The detected patterns are summarised below:

| Alert | Description | Amount (EUR) | Date |
|-------|-------------|-------------:|------|
| TM-ALERT-2024-0341 | 14 incoming SEPA credits from 12 different EU counterparties, all < EUR 10,000 (structuring indicator) | 128,440 cumulative | 1–15 Jul 2024 |
| TM-ALERT-2024-0388 | Same-day outbound transfer of EUR 115,000 to a company in a high-risk jurisdiction (listed FATF grey-list) | 115,000 | 17 Jul 2024 |
| TM-ALERT-2024-0412 | Two large cash deposits (EUR 9,800 and EUR 9,950) within 48 hours — just below reporting thresholds | 19,750 | 5–6 Aug 2024 |
| TM-ALERT-2024-0455 | International wire transfer of EUR 48,000 to a previously unknown beneficiary — no commercial purpose declared | 48,000 | 12 Aug 2024 |

**Aggregate suspicious activity total:** EUR 311,190 over 43 days.

## 4. PRIOR DUE DILIGENCE STATUS

Standard Customer Due Diligence (SCDD) was performed at onboarding. No EDD was conducted. The transaction pattern identified was not anticipated in the expected account activity profile documented at onboarding.

---

## 5. ANALYST RECOMMENDATION

The transaction pattern presents indicators of:
1. **Structuring / Smurfing** — multiple sub-threshold deposits designed to avoid cash reporting obligations.
2. **Trade-Based Money Laundering (TBML)** — outbound transfers to high-risk jurisdictions with no demonstrated commercial purpose.

**Recommendation:** File SAR with FIU-DE and freeze new outbound transactions (internal hold pending compliance review).
