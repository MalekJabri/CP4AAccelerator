## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

This Policy establishes IBK Group's approach to the protection of personal data in compliance with **Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)** and applicable national data protection legislation across all jurisdictions in which IBK Group operates (Germany, Spain, France, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Luxembourg).

This Policy applies to:
- All IBK Group entities (legal entities within the IBK banking and insurance group).
- All employees, contractors, temporary staff, and third parties acting on behalf of IBK Group.
- All personal data processed by or on behalf of IBK Group in any medium (electronic, paper, verbal).

## 2. DEFINITIONS

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Personal Data | Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. |
| Data Subject | The natural person to whom personal data relates. |
| Data Controller | IBK Group entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data. |
| Data Processor | Entity that processes personal data on behalf of a Data Controller. |
| Processing | Any operation performed on personal data (collection, storage, use, disclosure, deletion). |
| DPO | Data Protection Officer — IBK Group's designated data protection authority contact. |
| DPIA | Data Protection Impact Assessment. |

---

## 3. DATA PROTECTION PRINCIPLES

IBK Group processes personal data in accordance with the following principles (GDPR Art. 5):

| Principle | IBK Group Commitment |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Lawfulness, Fairness, Transparency | All processing must have a documented legal basis (Art. 6/9). Customers and employees receive clear privacy notices. |
| Purpose Limitation | Data collected for a specific purpose may not be used for incompatible purposes without a new legal basis. |
| Data Minimisation | Only personal data that is necessary for the stated purpose shall be collected and retained. |
| Accuracy | Personal data shall be kept accurate and up to date. Customers may request correction at any time. |
| Storage Limitation | Personal data shall not be retained longer than necessary — see IBK Data Retention Schedule (POL-RET-2024-001). |
| Integrity & Confidentiality | Appropriate technical and organisational measures shall be implemented to protect against unauthorised access, loss, or destruction. |
| Accountability | IBK Group shall demonstrate compliance through records, DPIAs, training, and audit. |

## 4. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING

IBK Group relies on the following legal bases for its main processing activities:

| Processing Activity | Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6) |
|--------------------|--------------------------|
| Customer onboarding & KYC | Art. 6(1)(b) — Contract; Art. 6(1)(c) — Legal obligation (AML, MiFID, CRR) |
| Credit assessment | Art. 6(1)(b) — Contract |
| Marketing to existing customers | Art. 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interests |
| Employee HR records | Art. 6(1)(b) — Contract; Art. 6(1)(c) — Legal obligation |
| Fraud prevention | Art. 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interests |
| Regulatory reporting | Art. 6(1)(c) — Legal obligation |
| Automated profiling / credit scoring | Art. 6(1)(b) + Art. 22 safeguards |

---

## 5. SPECIAL CATEGORIES OF DATA (GDPR Art. 9)

IBK Group processes special categories of personal data only where:
- **Explicit consent** has been obtained from the data subject; or
- Processing is **necessary for employment, social security, or social protection law** purposes; or
- Processing is **necessary for archiving, research, or statistical purposes** in the public interest.

IBK Group's primary exposure is **health data** (insurance underwriting for life and health products) processed under Art. 9(2)(a) (explicit consent) and Art. 9(2)(b) (employment/social protection).

## 6. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

| Right | IBK Group Commitment |
|-------|---------------------|
| Right of Access (Art. 15) | Respond within 30 days; no charge for first request. |
| Right to Rectification (Art. 16) | Correct inaccurate data without undue delay. |
| Right to Erasure (Art. 17) | Erase where no overriding legal obligation to retain exists. |
| Right to Restriction (Art. 18) | Restrict processing during disputes. |
| Right to Data Portability (Art. 20) | Provide data in machine-readable format for automated processing. |
| Right to Object (Art. 21) | Honour objections to direct marketing; assess legitimate interests objections. |
| Rights relating to automated decisions (Art. 22) | Provide human review of automated credit decisions. |

## 7. DATA BREACH MANAGEMENT

IBK Group shall manage data breaches in accordance with the **IBK Data Breach Response Procedure (PROC-COMP-BREACH-001)**:

1. **Detection & Assessment:** Immediate escalation to the Information Security team and DPO.
2. **Containment:** Steps to stop ongoing breach within 1 hour of detection.
3. **Authority Notification:** Report to competent supervisory authority within **72 hours** where breach is likely to result in risk to individuals (GDPR Art. 33).
4. **Data Subject Notification:** Notify affected individuals without undue delay where breach is likely to result in high risk (GDPR Art. 34).
5. **Documentation:** All breaches (whether notifiable or not) to be documented in the IBK Breach Register.

---

## 8. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Transfers of personal data outside the EEA shall only occur where:
- An **adequacy decision** exists for the destination country (GDPR Art. 45); or
- **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC)** (EC Decision 2021/914) are in place; or
- **Binding Corporate Rules (BCR)** are approved by the lead supervisory authority.
