## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

IBK Internal Audit conducted a full-scope review of the Retail Credit Origination process covering the period 1 January 2023 to 31 March 2024. The audit assessed controls over credit application processing, affordability assessment, KYC documentation, and approval authority limits.

## 2. SCOPE & METHODOLOGY

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Population Tested | 342 retail loan applications (approved and declined) — Jan 2023 to Mar 2024 |
| Sample Size | 85 files (stratified random sample — 25% of population) |
| Testing Methods | Document review, system walkthrough (FLEX LOS), management interviews, data analytics |
| Standards Applied | IBK Credit Policy Manual v2022; CRR Article 79; EBA Guidelines on Loan Origination (EBA/GL/2020/06) |

---

## 3. FINDINGS

### Finding 1 — HIGH — Incomplete Income Verification on 18% of Sample Files

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Finding ID | AUD-2024-003-F01 |
| Severity | **High** |
| Description | 15 of 85 files reviewed (17.6%) did not contain a third-party income verification document (payslip, employer letter, or tax return) at the time of loan approval. IBK Credit Policy §4.2 requires income verification for all loans > EUR 5,000. |
| Root Cause | Manual workaround in FLEX LOS allowing override of the mandatory document checklist when a verbal income declaration was obtained by the adviser. |
| Potential Impact | Regulatory non-compliance (EBA/GL/2020/06 §96–98); increased credit risk exposure; potential mis-selling risk. |
| Management Response | Head of Retail Banking: "We accept the finding. FLEX LOS will be updated to remove the manual override. Retrospective review of affected files to be completed by 31 August 2024." |
| Target Remediation Date | 31 August 2024 |
| Remediation Owner | Thomas Weber, Head of Retail Credit |

---

### Finding 2 — MEDIUM — Approval Authority Limits Exceeded (3 Cases)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Finding ID | AUD-2024-003-F02 |
| Severity | **Medium** |
| Description | 3 loans (aggregate EUR 186,000) were approved by Branch Advisers outside their delegated authority (individual limit EUR 50,000). Dual approval from the Regional Credit Manager was not obtained. |
| Root Cause | Absence of automated authority limit enforcement in FLEX LOS for joint applications (where individual and co-applicant amounts are assessed separately). |
| Management Response | Accepted. System fix and immediate retraining of Branch Advisers to be completed by 30 September 2024. |
| Target Remediation Date | 30 September 2024 |

---

### Finding 3 — MEDIUM — KYC Refresh Overdue on 22% of Active Borrowers

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Finding ID | AUD-2024-003-F03 |
| Severity | **Medium** |
| Description | 19 of 85 active borrower files reviewed had KYC review dates that were overdue by more than 6 months (up to 14 months overdue). IBK AML Policy §3.4 requires Standard CDD refresh every 3 years. |
| Root Cause | No automated trigger in the KYC management system to alert RMs when refresh is approaching. |
| Management Response | Accepted. CRM system to be updated with automated reminder workflow by 31 October 2024. |
| Target Remediation Date | 31 October 2024 |

---

### Finding 4 — MEDIUM — Property Valuation Reports Not Filed for 8% of Mortgage Files

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Finding ID | AUD-2024-003-F04 |
| Severity | **Medium** |
| Description | 7 of 85 mortgage files did not contain a final valuation report — only a preliminary estimate was on file. IBK Mortgage Policy §7.1 requires the final signed valuation to be filed prior to first drawdown. |
| Management Response | Accepted. Retrospective filing and process re-training underway. |
| Target Remediation Date | 15 August 2024 |

## 5. AUDIT OPINION

Based on the work performed, IBK Internal Audit issues a **"Needs Improvement"** opinion for Retail Credit Origination. No Critical findings were identified. The High finding (F01) represents a material control gap relative to regulatory requirements and must be remediated as a priority. Internal Audit will follow up on all outstanding actions by **31 October 2024** and report status to the Audit Committee.
