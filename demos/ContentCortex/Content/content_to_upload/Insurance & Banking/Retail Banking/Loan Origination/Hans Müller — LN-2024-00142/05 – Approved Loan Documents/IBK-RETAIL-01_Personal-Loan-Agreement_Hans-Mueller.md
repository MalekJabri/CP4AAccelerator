## 2. DISBURSEMENT

The Lender shall disburse the Loan Amount of **EUR 25,000** to the Borrower's current account (Account No. DE89 5001 0517 1234 5678 90) within **5 business days** of execution of this Agreement and fulfilment of all conditions precedent.

---

## 3. REPAYMENT

Repayments shall be collected by direct debit on the **15th of each calendar month**, commencing on **15 May 2024**. The Borrower authorises IBK Bank AG to debit the repayment account. Each instalment of **EUR 474.93** comprises principal and interest components as set out in the amortisation schedule (Annex A).

---

## 4. EARLY REPAYMENT

The Borrower may make early partial or full repayment at any time. A prepayment fee of **0.5% of the prepaid amount** applies where the remaining loan term exceeds 12 months, in accordance with applicable consumer credit legislation.

---

## 5. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

The following shall constitute events of default:

1. Non-payment of any instalment within **30 days** of the due date.
2. Material misrepresentation in the loan application.
3. Borrower becoming subject to insolvency proceedings.
4. Breach of any material covenant under this Agreement.

Upon an event of default, IBK Bank AG may declare the outstanding loan balance immediately due and payable.
