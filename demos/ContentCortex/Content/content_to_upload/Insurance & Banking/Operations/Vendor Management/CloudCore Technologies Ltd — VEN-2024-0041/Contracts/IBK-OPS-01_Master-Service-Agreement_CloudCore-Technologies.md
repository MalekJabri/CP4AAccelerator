## 1. SERVICES

The Vendor agrees to provide the following services (the "Services") to IBK:

| Service | Description | SLA Tier |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Cloud Infrastructure | AWS-equivalent managed private cloud hosting for IBK non-production and production environments | Tier 1 |
| Managed Security Operations (SOC) | 24/7 security monitoring, SIEM management, incident response | Tier 1 |
| Backup & Disaster Recovery | Daily incremental backup; RTO 4 hours; RPO 1 hour | Tier 1 |
| IT Service Desk | L1/L2 support — 08:00–20:00 CET Mon–Fri; L1 on-call outside hours | Tier 2 |
| Patch Management | Monthly OS and application patching per IBK change management policy | Tier 2 |

## 4. SECURITY & DATA PROTECTION

4.1 The Vendor shall maintain ISO 27001 certification throughout the term.  
4.2 The Vendor shall process IBK personal data solely as a Data Processor under a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA — Annex C), in accordance with GDPR Art. 28.  
4.3 All IBK data shall remain within the EEA. No sub-processing outside EEA without prior written IBK consent.  
4.4 The Vendor shall notify IBK of any security incident affecting IBK systems within **1 hour** of detection.  
4.5 Annual penetration testing of IBK environments by an IBK-approved third party at Vendor's cost.

---

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All work product, reports, and deliverables produced specifically for IBK under this Agreement shall vest in IBK upon creation. Pre-existing Vendor IP licenced to IBK on a non-exclusive basis for the duration of the Agreement.

---

## 6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

The Vendor's total aggregate liability shall not exceed **EUR 3,700,000** (200% of annual contract value) per contract year, except in cases of:
- Death or personal injury caused by negligence;
- Fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation;
- Data breaches resulting from Vendor's gross negligence.
