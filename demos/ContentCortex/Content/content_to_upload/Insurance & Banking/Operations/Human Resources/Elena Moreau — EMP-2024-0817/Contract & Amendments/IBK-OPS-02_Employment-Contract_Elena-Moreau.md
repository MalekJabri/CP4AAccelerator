## 4. CONFIDENTIALITY & NON-COMPETE

4.1 **Confidentiality:** The Employee shall keep confidential all non-public information relating to IBK and its clients during and for 2 years after the termination of employment.

4.2 **Non-compete:** For a period of **12 months** following termination, the Employee agrees not to join a direct competitor of IBK within the European banking & fintech sector. IBK will pay 50% of last base salary during the non-compete period in lieu.

4.3 **Non-solicitation:** For 12 months following termination, the Employee shall not solicit IBK clients or employees.
