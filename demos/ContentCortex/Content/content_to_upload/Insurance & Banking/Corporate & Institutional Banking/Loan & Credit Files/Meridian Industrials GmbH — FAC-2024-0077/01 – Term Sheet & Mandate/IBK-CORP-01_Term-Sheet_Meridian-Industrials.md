## 4. SECURITY PACKAGE

- First-ranking pledge over shares of Meridian Industrials GmbH and Meridian Manufacturing GmbH.
- First-ranking fixed and floating charge over all assets of the Borrower.
- First-ranking assignment of material contracts and insurance policies.
- Guarantee from Meridian Industrials Holding AG (EUR 150m, unconditional and irrevocable).

---

## 5. FINANCIAL COVENANTS (tested semi-annually)

| Covenant | Threshold |
|---------|-----------|
| Net Leverage Ratio (Net Debt / Adjusted EBITDA) | ≤ 3.50x (stepping down to 3.00x from Year 3) |
| Interest Cover Ratio (Adjusted EBITDA / Net Finance Charges) | ≥ 4.00x |
| Minimum Liquidity | EUR 10,000,000 at all times |

---

## 6. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

1. Satisfactory completion of legal, financial, and tax due diligence.
2. Delivery of audited financial statements for FY2022 and FY2023.
3. Receipt of all necessary corporate authorisations (board resolutions, shareholder approvals).
4. Satisfactory KYC / AML / EDD on all obligors.
5. No Material Adverse Change.
